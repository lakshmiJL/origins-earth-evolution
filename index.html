How the index is estimated

Each fit is clamped to 0–100: temperature is 100 − 3.1 × distance from 19°C; rainfall is 100 − 0.55 × distance from 100 cm/year; oxygen is 100 − 5 × distance from 21%. Diversity contributes its selected value directly. The final index is the rounded weighted sum shown below, not a prediction of a real ecosystem.
